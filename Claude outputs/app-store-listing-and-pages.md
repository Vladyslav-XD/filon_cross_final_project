Mocktail Finder helps you discover, save and create alcohol-free drinks — for a dry month, a party where everyone should feel included, or simply a better glass of something on a weeknight.

## 2. Скріншоти

Обов'язковий набір — iPhone 6,9″: 1320 × 2868 px, портрет, PNG або JPG без прозорості, від 1 до 10 штук. Менші iPhone Apple масштабує з цього набору. iPad не потрібен (`supportsTablet: false`).

Перші три видно без прокрутки в App Store — вони мають продати застосунок самі. Рекомендую 6 штук у такому порядку:

| # | Екран у застосунку | Підпис (headline) | Навіщо |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Головна: шапка, пошук, категорії, картки рецептів | **100+ alcohol-free drinks** | Одразу відповідає «що це» |
| 2 | Деталі рецепта: фото, інгредієнти, кроки | **Ingredients and steps. Nothing else.** | Показує головну цінність — ясність |
| 3 | Головна з активними фільтрами (наприклад Citrus + Mint) | **Filter by taste or ingredient** | Показує глибину каталогу |
| 4 | Екран випадкового рецепта | **Can't decide? Get a surprise** | Емоційна фіча, гарний візуал |
| 5 | Улюблені з кількома збереженими | **Keep the ones you love** | Персональна колекція |
| 6 | Add Recipe (заповнена форма) у темній темі | **Add your own. Light or dark.** | Дві фічі одним кадром |

Практичні поради:
- Знімки з симулятора: обери iPhone 17 Pro Max (6,9″), у вікні симулятора Cmd+S — PNG зберігається на Desktop у потрібній роздільності.
- Оформлення в Figma: фрейм 1320 × 2868, фон — фірмовий градієнт (#00BBA7 → #0092B8) або світлий нейтральний, телефон у рамці зі скріншотом, заголовок Sora Bold ~110–120 px зверху. Один стиль для всіх шести.
- Заголовки до 4–5 слів, без крапок, крім випадків із двома реченнями.
- Перед завантаженням перевір, що на скріншотах немає статус-бару з реальним часом і низьким зарядом — у симуляторі він завжди 9:41 і 100%.

## 3. Privacy Policy — сторінка для vladfilon.com

Пропонована адреса: `https://www.vladfilon.com/mocktail-finder-privacy.html` (у тому ж стилі, що й кейс `mocktail-finder.html`). Ця адреса піде в поле Privacy Policy URL в App Store Connect.

```
Mocktail Finder — Privacy Policy

Effective date: [дата публікації]

Mocktail Finder is an iOS app by Vladyslav Filon that helps you discover, save and create non-alcoholic drink recipes. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the app does not collect any personal data.

What the app stores on your device
Your favourite recipes and the recipes you create yourself are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are not uploaded anywhere and are never seen by us. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. It may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer backups) according to your own iOS settings.

Information sent over the internet
To show recipes and photos, the app requests them from TheCocktailDB (thecocktaildb.com), a third-party recipe database. As with any internet request, TheCocktailDB's servers receive standard technical information such as your device's IP address. We do not receive, store or have access to this information. TheCocktailDB's own privacy practices are described on its website.

If you add an image link to your own recipe, the app loads that image from the address you provided. If you leave the field empty, a default image is loaded from Unsplash.

What the app does not do
- No account or sign-up.
- No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
- No collection of names, email addresses, contacts, location or any other personal data.
- No data sold or shared with anyone.

Sharing recipes
When you use "Share Recipe", the app hands the recipe text to the iOS share sheet. What happens next is controlled by the app you choose to share with (Messages, Mail and so on) and by your own choices.

Children
The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date changed. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact
Questions about privacy: [email]
```

## 4. Support — сторінка для vladfilon.com

Пропонована адреса: `https://www.vladfilon.com/mocktail-finder-support.html` → поле Support URL в App Store Connect.

```
Mocktail Finder — Support

Thanks for using Mocktail Finder. If something isn't working or you have an idea, write to [email] — I read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. The app works without sign-up, and there are no ads.

Where are my favourites and my own recipes stored?
On your device only. They survive app updates, but they are removed if you delete the app. They are not synced between devices yet.

Recipes won't load. What can I do?
Recipes come from an online database, so the app needs an internet connection. Check your connection and tap "Try again". If the problem continues, please email me with your iPhone model and iOS version.

Can I add a photo to my own recipe?
In this version you can paste a link to an image. Choosing a photo from your library is planned for a future update.

How do I share a recipe?
Open any recipe and tap "Share Recipe". The recipe is sent as text with ingredients and steps through any app you choose.

Is the app free?
Yes, completely.

Privacy Policy: https://www.vladfilon.com/mocktail-finder-privacy.html
```
